F1. Analytics

A historical data dashboard for Formula 1 — built for fans, analysts, and developers who want more than a scoreboard.

Project

What is this?

F1. Analytics is an open, browser-based dashboard that pulls live historical data from two public Formula 1 APIs — no account, no subscription, no paywall. It covers every season from 2000 to 2025, with race results, qualifying times, driver and constructor standings, pit stop telemetry, and five interactive charts per season.

The goal is to make 25 years of F1 data explorable in one place, with a clean interface that stays out of the way of the data.

26 Seasons covered
500+ Races indexed
5 Charts per season
2 Live data sources

Features

What's included

Race Results

Full race-by-race results for every season, including winner, team, grid position, and date. Scrollable inline list, no pagination.

Qualifying

Pole position per round with Q1/Q2/Q3 lap times where available, going back to the introduction of knockout qualifying in 2006.

Driver & Constructor Standings

Final championship standings for every season, with points, wins, and team colour coding matched to each constructor's livery.

Pit Stop Telemetry

Live pit stop data from the OpenF1 API for the most recent race of each season — total stops, average duration, fastest stop, and max stops per driver.

5 Analytics Charts

Championship battle (cumulative points), race wins by driver, constructor share donut, grid vs finish scatter, and positions gained/lost per race for the champion.

Dark Mode

Full light/dark theme with system preference detection and localStorage persistence. Charts, tooltips, and all UI elements switch cleanly.
